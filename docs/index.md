# LaTeX Collaborative Editor

Welcome. This guide explains how to use the **LaTeX Collaborative Editor** in the browser: writing LaTeX with a live preview, working with others in real time, and saving your work locally or in Google Drive.

## What you can do

- **Edit LaTeX** in a full editor with syntax-aware feedback when something goes wrong in the preview pipeline.
- **See a formatted preview** that updates as you type (with a short debounce so typing stays smooth).
- **Collaborate** by sharing a link: peers connect directly for editing; the document content stays between you and your collaborators after the room is joined.
- **Save** to the browser’s local storage, download a `.tex` file, or connect **Google Drive** to open and save files in your account (when the host has configured it).
