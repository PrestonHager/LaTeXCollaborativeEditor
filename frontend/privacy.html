Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 28, 2026

Collaborative LaTeX Editor is designed to keep document content peer-to-peer between collaborators. Signaling infrastructure is used only to exchange connection metadata needed to establish WebRTC sessions.

What We Process

  • Document text is exchanged directly between peers when collaboration is active.
  • Connection metadata (room IDs, SDP, ICE) is relayed by signaling services.
  • If you connect Google Drive, file contents are sent to Google APIs for autosave.

What We Do Not Store Centrally

  • No server-side document database is used by this application.
  • No account system is required for basic peer collaboration.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the project maintainer through the repository issue tracker.
